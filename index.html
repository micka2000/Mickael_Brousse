Professional Journey

May 2024 — Nov 2025 Banking

Data Governance Analyst VIE

BGL-BNP Paribas · Luxembourg

  • Overhauled the bank’s Personal Data Processing Register (GDPR, equivalent to Quebec’s Law 25): 180 processing activities reviewed with process owners across every business line, about 52 privacy assessments a year.
  • Built the Power BI KPI dashboard used to track those assessments and steer the quarterly compliance reviews.
  • Cut the SAS-BDN Data Dictionary error rate from about 20% to about 5%: audited every table and column address via SQL on Oracle, removed duplicate definitions, enforced one definition per data address and added data quality indicators on every new entry.
  • Presented the dictionary and data-protection work to the bank’s data teams to converge on a single dictionary structure, laying the groundwork for AI-ready, context-rich definitions.

Sept 2022 — Aug 2023 Industry

Business Analyst / Data Owner

Michelin · Clermont-Ferrand, France

  • Acted as PMO for the Manufacturing data governance program (70 plants): co-built the annual roadmap of 36 data projects and aligned every Data Owner behind its 3 group objectives.
  • Tracked delivery on a Power BI dashboard whose velocity curve quickly showed the annual target would be missed.
  • Owned the 3,000-row matrix assigning every data definition to a business owner and its contributors, wrote the rules that kept it clean, and formalized the Data Definition process through an interactive alignment workshop.
  • Designed and piloted a Collibra workflow to move that matrix into the group data catalog, running design sessions in English with the COE team in Pune, India, and applied change management (ADKAR, ADEQUA) to the blockers slowing catalog adoption.
  • Replaced a manual monthly follow-up across plants with an automated progress report for the steering committee, freeing about 1 day a month for the data owner, and rebuilt the underlying data with plant experts into the group’s master list of machine types.
